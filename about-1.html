Heran is a writer and producer of fiction, film and new media projects. Born in Addis Ababa and based in Brooklyn, her creative practice seeks to break through borders real and imagined.

She has won an Emmy for {The And}, a short doc series she produced. Her writing is rooted in told stories, fieldwork and an original archive of photographs, letters and found objects gathered over a decade of collaborative research.

Her short fiction has been published in Michigan Quarterly Review, Kweli Journal and the Caine Prize Anthology among others. Her work has been supported by the New York Foundation for the Arts (NYFA), Djerassi Resident Artists Program (California), the Caine Prize Residency (Rwanda) and Kwani? Writing Workshop (Kenya).
